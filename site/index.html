01

The problem

Enterprises store data well and connect it poorly. The signal that predicted fraud, failure, or churn often already exists — distributed across systems that never meet in time.

02

The proposal

A data lake stores. A Data Swimming Pool connects: relationships between events become durable, scored, and policy-governed assets of the platform itself.

03

The posture

This is an original conceptual architecture for research and critique — not a product, and not an industry standard. Limitations are treated as first-class content.
