01

The problem

Organisations store data brilliantly and connect it badly. The clue that predicted the fraud, the failure, or the churn already exists — in four different systems, noticed by nobody.

02

The idea

A data lake stores. A Data Swimming Pool connects: relationships between events become saved, scored, governed assets instead of one-off spreadsheet joins.

03

The honesty

This is a proposed design, not a product and not an industry standard. A whole chapter is devoted to why you might reasonably decide not to build it.
